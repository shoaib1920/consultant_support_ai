## 2. What VisaGuard is worth

All figures are estimates for the Pakistani market. Adjust them after your first 3–5 conversations.

**Cost to build it from scratch:** about 120 source files, with four countries' rules, a 28-day bank-statement analyser, AI document reading, approval gates, an audit trail, a student portal and 81 automated tests. For an experienced developer that is roughly **4–6 months of work**: about **PKR 30–50 lakh** at a Lahore software house, or **USD 30–60k** from a foreign agency.

**How to sell it (recommended): monthly subscription (SaaS)**

| Plan | For | Price / month |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 branch, up to 5 staff | PKR 15,000 – 20,000 |
| Growth | up to 15 staff, AI document reading | PKR 30,000 – 40,000 |
| Branches | multiple branches, priority support | PKR 60,000+ |

- Add a one-time setup and training fee of **PKR 50,000 – 1,00,000**. This covers loading their programmes and verifying their rules.
- Offer 2 months free for an annual prepayment.
- Alternative: sell a **white-label licence with source code** for **PKR 15–25 lakh** to one large consultancy. Only do this if you don't want to sell to others.

**The pitch:** the cost of the software is small next to the cost of a single mistake. One non-refundable deposit paid for an ineligible student (e.g. £3,000 ≈ PKR 10+ lakh), or one refusal caused by funds dipping below the requirement, costs more than a year of subscription and damages the consultancy's reputation.

## 3. How much work it handles

- **Everything from intake to decision:** student intake (including a secure intake link the student fills in), the case file, the document checklist, eligibility, finances, attestation timelines, fee payments, approvals, tasks, reports and the audit log.
- **Staff roles:** Owner, Manager, Officer and Student portal, each with its own permissions.
- **Volume:** comfortably handles a consultancy with **hundreds of active cases and 10–20+ staff**.
  - On Firebase's free plan, the practical limit is file storage (about 1 GB, so roughly a thousand uploaded documents).
  - For paying clients, move to Firebase's pay-as-you-go plan. It costs a few dollars a month at that size and removes the limit.
- **Every night it automatically re-checks all open cases** for expiring rules, approaching deadlines and stale information.
- **Uploads:** up to 4 MB each (PDF or images), stored encrypted.

## 4. Problems it solves (use these in conversation)

1. **Ineligible students reaching payment:** CGPA below the minimum, missing English test, wrong degree. VisaGuard flags the case **RED** and **blocks fee and deposit payments** until a manager approves.
2. **Bank-statement refusals:**
   - checks the 28-day rule day by day (e.g. "funds dropped below £22,539 on day 19")
   - flags unexplained large deposits
   - checks that the statement is less than 31 days old
   - converts PKR into the required currency.
3. **Missed deadlines:** tracks the attestation chain (HEC → MOFA → APS for Germany; Universitaly, DoV and CIMEA for Italy) and warns when it will finish after the university or embassy deadline.
4. **Inconsistent documents:** a name spelled differently on the passport and the degree, or dates that don't match. AI reads the documents and highlights the mismatches.
5. **Officer mistakes and fraud risk:** any payment needs **two people to confirm** (officer + manager). Every action is recorded in a **tamper-proof audit log**.
6. **Outdated country rules:** every funds or requirement rule has an official source and a "verified by manager" status. A case can never show GREEN on an unverified or expired rule.
7. **Chasing students:** a student portal for signing risk acknowledgements, a secure intake link, ready-made WhatsApp and email reminder templates, and automatic tasks per case.
8. **Owner visibility:** a dashboard of every case's status (RED / AMBER / GREEN), pending approvals, payments and overdue tasks.

Countries supported now: **UK, Germany, Italy, France**. More can be added (Ireland, for example) without rebuilding anything.

**Important:** never promise visa approval. VisaGuard reduces avoidable refusals and mistakes, and a human always makes the final decision.
