## 2. Meeting flow (25–30 minutes)

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 min | Greeting + a question | "Kya kabhi fee jama hone ke baad pata chala ke student eligible nahi tha?" Then **listen**. |
| 2–12 min | Slides 1–10 | Speak to each slide's notes. Don't read them word for word. |

## 3. Full speech (Roman Urdu)

### Slide 1: Opening
> Assalam-o-Alaikum. Bohat shukriya ke aap ne waqt diya. Main [naam], Devtimize se. Aaj main aap ko VisaGuard dikhaunga: study-visa consultancies ke liye software jo ghalat file par paisa zaya hone se **pehle** rok deta hai. Sirf 15-20 minute. Aakhir mein aap ke sawal, aur agar pasand aaye to 14 din ka free trial aap ke apne cases ke saath.
> Pehle aik sawal: kya aap ke office mein kabhi aisa hua ke fee ya deposit jama ho gaya, aur baad mein pata chala ke student eligible hi nahi tha?

*(Wait for the answer. Whatever they say, refer back to it later.)*

### Slide 2: The problem
> Yeh asal kahani hai. Lahore mein mere aik consultant dost ne sirf aik student ka CGPA check nahi kiya. Fee jama ho gayi, aik hi din mein 20,000 rupay zaya hue, aur student bhi naraz ho kar kisi aur consultancy chala gaya.
> Aur yeh akeli ghalti nahi. Char sab se aam nuqsan: eligibility se pehle fee, 28 din mein balance ka kam hona, attestation ka deadline ke baad mukammal hona, aur documents mein naam ya date ka farq.
> Yeh bad-qismati nahi, process ki ghaltiyan hain. Aur process ki ghalti software rok sakta hai. In mein se aap ke office mein sab se zyada kaun si hoti hai?

### Slide 3: Stages
> VisaGuard mein har file 11 stages se guzarti hai, bilkul airport security ki tarah: jab tak har check clear na ho, aagay nahi ja sakte. Teen stages laal hain: Payment, Submission, Visa Filing. Yeh woh kaam hain jo wapas nahi ho sakte, aur yeh tab tak band rehte hain jab tak har check pass na ho. Owner bhi gate skip nahi kar sakta, aur har kaam ka record rehta hai.

### Slide 4: Traffic light
> Har file ko aik rang milta hai, wajah aur hal ke saath. GREEN: sab checks pass. Lekin hum kabhi nahi kehte ke visa pakka hai. AMBER: kuch dekhna hai. RED: rokne wala masla, payment band.
> Misaal dekhein: CGPA 2.45, minimum 3.00. Software saaf likhta hai: fee mat bharein. Yahi woh 20,000 wali ghalti hai jo yahan ruk jati.

### Slide 5: Two-person approval
> Har non-refundable payment par do log zaroori hain. Officer request karta hai, aik **doosra** shakhs, manager, confirm karta hai, aur usay laal banner mein nazar aata hai ke kitne lakh khatre mein hain. Confirm karte waqt software sab dobara check karta hai. Is se ghalti bhi rukti hai aur staff ki taraf se ghalat istemal bhi.

### Slide 6: Automatic checks
> Chhe cheezen software khud check karta hai: CGPA aur English, bank statement ka 28-din test din-ba-din, documents ka aapas mein milan, attestation timelines, har mulk ki zaroriyat, aur sirf verified rules.

### Slide 7: Official website import (new)
> Yeh feature aik consultancy owner ki request par bana. Ab aap sirf university ki official programme page ka link paste karte hain. Software khud parhta hai (CGPA, IELTS, fees, intake, deadline), aur har value ke saath official page ka exact jumla bhi dikhata hai. Aap aik dafa check kar ke save karte hain. Aur har raat software dobara page parhta hai: agar university ne IELTS ya fees badli, to aap ko alert, aur aik click mein update.

### Slide 8: University suggestions (new)
> Student ka profile bharte hi software behtareen universities suggest karta hai: pasandida course, CGPA, IELTS, budget aur open intake dekh kar, har suggestion ke saath wajah aur kharcha rupay mein. Jab transcript ya IELTS result verify hota hai, list khud update ho jati hai. Yeh andaaza hai, guarantee nahi.

### Slide 9: AI + humans
> AI parhta hai aur batata hai. Faisla hamesha aap ka staff karta hai. AI kabhi approve nahi karta, payment nahi karta. Aur agar kisi document mein AI ko dhoka dene wala chhupa text ho, software usay pakar leta hai.

### Slide 10: Trust, 102 tests
> Software mein **102 automated tests** hain, aur sab pass hain. Har check (payment gate, 28-din rule, fee block, security) computer khud baar baar check karta hai, har update se pehle. Audit log jo koi edit nahi kar sakta, har staff ka role, documents encrypted, aur aap ka data sirf aap ka.

### Live demo (section 4), then slide 11: Getting started
> Shuru karna aik din ka kaam hai. Staff aur rules, phir aap ki universities ke links, phir 2-3 asal students. 14ve din faisla. Kuch install nahi karna: browser mein chalta hai, mobile par bhi.

### Slide 12: Pricing
> Mahana qeemat: Starter 18,000, Growth 35,000 (zyada tar yehi leti hain), Branches 60,000 se. Setup aur training aik dafa 50,000. Saal ka ikattha dein to 2 mahine free. Aur pehle 14 din bilkul free.
> Hisaab seedha hai: agar yeh saal mein sirf aik ghalat deposit rok le, to poore saal ki fees nikal aati hai.

### Slide 13: Close
> Meri guzarish: is hafte free trial shuru karein. Aap 2-3 live students chunein, hum aik baithak mein setup kar dete hain, aur 14ve din mil kar dekhte hain. Kya Thursday 11 baje ya Saturday 12 baje theek rahega?

**After asking, stay silent.** Whoever speaks first after the question loses ground.

## 5. Questions they may ask, with answers

### About the product

**Q: Yeh exactly kya karta hai, simple words mein?**
A: Har student ki file ko stages mein chalata hai, har step par check karta hai (CGPA, English, bank statement, documents, deadlines), aur paisa jama karne ya application bhejne se pehle rok deta hai agar koi masla ho.

**Q: Kya yeh visa approve karwa dega?**
A: Nahi, aur koi software yeh wada nahi kar sakta. Yeh **avoidable ghaltiyan** rokta hai: ghalat fee, kam balance, deadline miss, documents ka farq. Faisla embassy ka hai.

**Q: Kaun se mulk?**
A: Abhi UK, Germany, Italy, France. Naya mulk (Ireland, Hungary, Canada) add ho sakta hai. Code mein yeh aik tayyar tareeqa hai, aam tor par kuch din ka kaam. *(Don't promise a date before checking with the developer.)*

**Q: Hamare apne universities aur programmes?**
A: Universities page par official link paste karein: software page parh kar requirements bhar deta hai. Har raat dobara check karta hai aur changes par alert deta hai. Haath se bhi add kar sakte hain.

**Q: Universities ki suggestions kaise deta hai?**
A: Student ke pasandida course, CGPA, IELTS, budget aur open intakes dekh kar aap ke database ke programmes ko rank karta hai, wajah aur kharche ke saath. Suggestions sirf unhi programmes se hoti hain jo aap ne system mein daale hain.

**Q: Student khud details bhar sakta hai?**
A: Haan. Secure link WhatsApp par bhejein, student ghar se bhar deta hai. Student portal bhi hai jahan woh risk acknowledgement sign karta hai.

**Q: WhatsApp par khud message bhejta hai?**
A: **Abhi nahi.** Ready-made WhatsApp aur email reminder templates hain jo staff copy kar ke bhejta hai. Automatic sending baad mein add ho sakti hai.

**Q: Mobile app hai?**
A: Alag app nahi, lekin website mobile browser mein chalti hai. Kuch install nahi karna.

**Q: University portal par application khud submit karta hai?**
A: Nahi. Submission aap ka staff university ke portal par karta hai. VisaGuard yeh check karta hai ke submit karna **mehfooz** hai, aur record rakhta hai.

**Q: Bank statement kaise check hota hai?**
A: Statement upload karein ya entries daalein. Software har din ka balance required amount se milata hai, batata hai kis din aur kitna kam hua, aur bari bina-wajah deposits flag karta hai.

### About AI

**Q: AI kaun sa hai?**
A: Google ka Gemini (ya Anthropic ka Claude). Documents se details nikalta hai aur university pages parhta hai.

**Q: Agar AI ghalat parhe?**
A: Isi liye har document staff verify karta hai, aur university page ki har value ke saath official jumla dikhaya jata hai, taa ke aap foran check kar saken. AI kabhi koi faisla, approval ya payment nahi karta.

**Q: AI ki limit khatam ho gayi to?**
A: Software rozana ki had khud sambhalta hai. Agar had poori ho jaye, staff fields haath se bhar deta hai. Kaam nahi rukta.

### Data, security and privacy

**Q: Hamara data kahan rehta hai?**
A: Google ke cloud (Firebase) mein, encrypted. Har consultancy ka data alag. Doosri consultancy aap ka data nahi dekh sakti.

**Q: Aap (Devtimize) hamara data dekh sakte hain?**
A: Technically system admin ke paas database ki access hoti hai, jaise har software company ke paas. Hum aap ke data ko kisi aur maqsad ke liye istemal nahi karte, aur yeh contract mein likh dete hain. *(Offer a simple data-protection clause in the agreement.)*

**Q: Kya student ke passport aur bank statement AI ko bheje jate hain?**
A: Haan, details nikalne ke liye document AI ko bheja jata hai. **Important:** live clients ke liye hum Gemini ka **paid** plan ya Claude istemal karenge, jin ki terms ke mutabiq aap ka data unke models train karne mein istemal nahi hota. *(See section 7 about the free key.)*

**Q: Staff data chura le ya delete kar de to?**
A: Har staff ka role hai, aur har kaam audit log mein jata hai jo edit ya delete nahi ho sakta. Student ka data sirf **owner** delete kar sakta hai, poora naam type kar ke, aur deletion ka record rehta hai.

**Q: Backup?**
A: Data Google Firebase par hai. Har student ka poora data JSON mein export ho sakta hai. Rozana automatic poore database ka backup Firebase ke paid plan par set hota hai: live clients ke liye hum yeh on karenge. *(This is not set up yet; set it up before your first paying client.)*

**Q: Student ki consent?**
A: Intake par consent liya aur record hota hai (version ke saath). Student ka data export ya delete kiya ja sakta hai.

### Pricing and contract

**Q: Mehenga hai.**
A: Aik ghalat non-refundable deposit aam tor par 5 se 15 lakh ka hota hai, aur aik kho jane wala client alag. Software ka saal ka kharcha is se kam hai. Aur 14 din free try karein: agar aik bhi ghalti pakre, tab faisla karein.

**Q: Discount?**
A: Saal ka ikattha dene par 2 mahine free. *(Your floor price is decided in advance; don't go below it in the room.)*

**Q: Contract kitne arse ka?**
A: Mahana, kabhi bhi band karein. Annual par discount.

**Q: Band kar dein to data?**
A: Aap ka data aap ka hai. Export kar ke de dete hain.

**Q: Hidden charges?**
A: Nahi. Mahana fee + aik dafa setup. Agar bohat zyada AI istemal ho (hazaron documents), us ka kharcha pehle se bata denge.

### Setup, training and support

**Q: Staff seekh lega?**
A: Haan: setup ke din training, aur har screen par saaf buttons aur wajah likhi hoti hai. Zyada tar staff pehle din hi chala leta hai.

**Q: Support?**
A: WhatsApp par, office hours mein. Growth plan par priority support.

**Q: Internet chala jaye?**
A: Software online hai, internet chahiye. Data cloud mein mehfooz rehta hai; internet wapas aate hi wahin se kaam.

**Q: Hamara purana data (Excel) daal sakte hain?**
A: Students aur programmes import karne mein hum setup ke waqt madad karte hain. *(Bulk Excel import isn't built yet; do it manually or build it if they insist.)*

### Trust and comparison

**Q: Hum Excel aur WhatsApp groups se kaam chala rahe hain. Kya farq?**
A: Excel yaad nahi dilata, rokta nahi. Woh ghalat fee jama hone deta hai. VisaGuard payment se pehle **rokta** hai, do log confirm karte hain, aur har kaam ka record rehta hai.

**Q: Aap ne kitni consultancies ko diya hai?**
A: Honest jawab: "Hum abhi launch kar rahe hain, aur pehle clients ko khaas qeemat aur zyada support de rahe hain. Isi liye 14 din free trial: khud dekh lein." **Never claim clients you don't have.**

**Q: 102 tests ka kya matlab?**
A: Software ke har ahem hisse (payment rokna, 28-din rule, documents ka milan, security) ko computer khud test karta hai. Har update se pehle 102 tests chalte hain, sab pass hon tab hi update jata hai.

**Q: Software ghalat bataye to zimmedari kis ki?**
A: VisaGuard decision-support tool hai, legal ya immigration advice nahi. Rules aap ke managers verify karte hain, aur faisla aap ka staff karta hai. Software ghaltiyan pakarne mein madad karta hai.

### Hard objections

- **"Abhi zaroorat nahi."** → "Bilkul. Bas 14 din free try karein, apne 2-3 live files ke saath. Agar kuch na pakre, to koi baat nahi."
- **"Sochenge."** → "Zaroor. Kya cheez sochni hai: qeemat, staff, ya data? Main abhi jawab de doon." Then "Main Thursday ko follow up karun?"
- **"Hamara staff already check karta hai."** → "Bilkul, aur yeh unki madad karta hai. Jis din koi busy ho ya naya ho, software phir bhi rok deta hai."

## 7. Before using the Gemini free key with real clients

- **Turning it on:** create a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. In Vercel, add `GEMINI_API_KEY` (and optionally `GEMINI_MODEL`), then redeploy. Settings → AI provider will show "Gemini (gemini-2.5-flash) · 0 / 200 AI calls used today".
- **Quota:** the app paces itself to 8 calls a minute and 200 a day by default. Change them with `AI_PROVIDER_RPM` and `AI_DAILY_LIMIT` if Google gives your key different limits.
- **Privacy (important):** Google's free-tier terms allow it to use what you send to improve its products. That's fine for the demo and for university web pages. **Before uploading real students' passports and bank statements**, move to Gemini's paid tier (same key, add billing; it costs very little at this volume) or Claude.
- **Model name:** `gemini-2.5-flash` is the default. If Google retires it, the app says "model was not found". Set `GEMINI_MODEL` to the current Flash model.
